Strikers Stadium Studio

Look around Mario Strikers Charged's stadiums, repaint them (the pitch, the stands, the sky, the signs), choose which fans cheer for each team, and make a mod that turns on in F10 > Mods.

Open your game's files folder

Click Open game folder and choose the extracted game's files folder. For the PC port it's here (paste it into the folder window's address bar):

%LOCALAPPDATA%\MSCRecomp\Game\files

The browser may ask to "upload" the files: nothing leaves your computer, the app reads them locally.

Nothing here changes your game files. Mods come out as downloads you install yourself.
